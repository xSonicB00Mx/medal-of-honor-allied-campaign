# High-resolution health HUD

Four coordinated textures replace the original 16x64 health sprites with 128x512 artwork: healthback (red), healthmeter (green), healthmeterflash (warm white), potentialhealthmeter (yellow healing preview, SH/BT only). Original rectangles, clipping, fade timing, labels, health values and gameplay remain unchanged. Unused health needles and healthback2ammo are excluded. No multiplayer-specific assets are changed.

Built-in Imagegen edit mode used the four extracted original sprites as references, with transparent background. Final prompt: restore four identical narrow vertical gauges in red/green/warm white/yellow, side by side; preserve the original thin aged brass/olive rim and inset luminous fill, straight orthographic geometry, subtle horizontal shading, no labels, ornaments, ticks or external glow. All four frames must have identical geometry. Selected master: generated-local/hud-upscale/health/generated/health-states.png, output exec-6dfa7ac3-3921-4ff3-ac8d-7ec94cddf955.png. Prepared textures: generated-local/hud-upscale/health/prepared. Source sprites: generated-local/hud-upscale/health/source. Assets stay local and excluded from Git.

tools/package_health_art.py extracts the four sheet columns and registers their opaque bounds to the existing 1:4 texture rectangles. Effective original shader definitions and alpha behavior are preserved, with mip filtering enabled. Health bars use normalized clipped UV coordinates, so no ammunition-style tile correction is needed. Package reports include crop bounds and master/archive hashes. No engine rebuild or UI layout edits.

tools/test_health_art.py uses isolated profiles, actual player health stats and the native engine at 3840x2160 / HUD 1.66. AA aa-r1 and SH sh-r1 passed: 100, 75, 50, 25, 1 and return to 100, each during transition and after settling. Visually inspected fill heights, red/green boundaries, rim alignment and white change flash. These scripted HUD fixtures are not combat or full mission acceptance. The yellow potential-health texture is packaged for the existing expansion widget; sustained gradual healing was not exercised (the current single-player engine applies queued healing immediately). Archive CRC/entry scope/dimensions, metadata, campaign and weapon-policy validators passed.
